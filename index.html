04 BIOCHEMICAL KINETIC ART

Carbonate drip sculpture — concept model

A contained, recirculating art-and-research concept: capture CO₂ from a gas stream, dissolve it into a controlled aqueous carbonate system, then deposit calcium carbonate on a replaceable nucleation scaffold. This is not a claim that soot or other solid carbon particles become limestone.

The estimate assumes captured CO₂ is fully converted to CaCO₃; real conversion, purity, scaling and losses must be measured.
Design boundary: keep the mineralization loop physically contained; monitor pH, alkalinity, calcium, conductivity, flow and off-gas CO₂. Do not discharge process water or release captured particulate matter without characterization and treatment.
